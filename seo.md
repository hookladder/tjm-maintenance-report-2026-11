# Redirects & links

As part of the post-launch clean-up I moved every redirect to one plugin and cleaned up the internal links.

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Old-site URLs redirected | **~480** permanent (301) redirects, each landing in one hop: menus, pages, 122 location pages (FR/EN), blog posts and promotions |
| Redirect source | Consolidated from Rank Math into **Redirection** |
| Internal links pointing at redirects or 404s | **~1,716 → ~347** (−80%) across headers, footers, templates and 21 posts |

Redirects are managed in **Tools → Redirection**. The plugin ignores query parameters, trailing slashes and letter case, so one rule covers every variant of an old URL.
